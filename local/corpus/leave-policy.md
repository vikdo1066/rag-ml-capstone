# Employee Leave Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026. This policy applies to all employees of Acme Corp on a United Kingdom or European Union contract. Contractors and agency workers are covered only where their agreement explicitly incorporates this document. Questions should be sent to people-ops@acme.example, not to a line manager’s personal inbox.


## 1. Annual paid time off
Full-time employees accrue 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays observed at their contracted location. Part-time employees accrue leave in proportion to contracted hours. Leave accrues monthly at 1/12 of the annual entitlement and may be taken before it has fully accrued, subject to manager approval and a negative balance of no more than five days. Unused leave may be carried into the next calendar year up to a maximum of five days; anything above that is forfeited unless People Operations has approved a written exception because operational needs prevented the employee from taking leave.

Employees with more than five years of continuous service receive two additional days. Employees with more than ten years receive five additional days. These service days do not carry over.


## 2. Requesting time off
Leave of three days or fewer should be requested in the HR system at least five working days in advance. Leave of more than three days should be requested at least fifteen working days in advance. Managers must respond within three working days. Competing requests for the same period are resolved by (1) operational coverage, (2) whose request was submitted first, then (3) seniority. Blackout periods may be declared for product launches or financial close; they will be published at least 30 days ahead and may not exceed 15 working days per year per team.

Unplanned absence must be reported to the manager by 09:30 local time on the first day, with an expected return date. Absence of more than seven consecutive calendar days requires a fit note or equivalent medical certificate.


## 3. Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year, separate from annual leave. After those days are exhausted, statutory sick pay rules of the contracted country apply. Recurring short absences (four or more episodes in a rolling quarter) trigger a wellbeing conversation, not automatic discipline. Acme Corp does not require employees to disclose diagnoses to their manager; occupational health may request information with the employee’s consent.


## 4. Parental and family leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave at full base salary, then 10 weeks at 50 percent of base salary, provided they have at least 26 weeks of service before the expected date of birth or placement. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of paid leave at full base salary, to be taken within 12 months of birth or placement. Adoption and surrogacy are treated the same as birth. Shared parental leave may be used to split remaining unpaid entitlement between eligible parents.

Employees may take up to 5 days of paid compassionate leave for the death of an immediate family member, and up to 3 days of unpaid leave for other close relationships. Carer’s leave of up to 5 unpaid days per year is available to provide care for a dependant with a long-term need.


## 5. Other leave
Jury service, court attendance, and reserve forces duties are paid for up to 10 working days per year; beyond that, statutory rules apply. Unpaid career breaks of 4 to 12 weeks may be requested after two years of service and are granted at the company’s discretion. Time off for antenatal appointments is paid for the primary caregiver and for one appointment for the secondary caregiver.


## 6. Recording and abuse
All leave must be recorded in the HR system. Taking leave that was not approved, or repeatedly failing to report absence, is a conduct issue under the Code of Conduct. This policy does not reduce statutory rights. Where statute is more generous than this document, statute wins.
