# Acme Corp Remote Work Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026. Acme Corp is hybrid by default. This policy explains where people may work, how often they should be in a company office, and what the company provides. It applies to employees. Contractors follow their statement of work unless this policy is incorporated.


## 1. Work location
Each employee has a contracted location (city and country) used for tax, right-to-work, and employment law. You may not permanently relocate without a written change of contract. Working from another country for more than 10 working days in a rolling quarter requires approval from People Operations and Tax, because permanent establishment and social security rules can be triggered.

Within the contracted country, employees in the “Hybrid” band are expected in a designated Acme office at least 2 days per week on average, measured over a calendar quarter. Teams may set core days (for example Tuesday and Wednesday) so collaboration is predictable. The “Remote” band is for roles posted as remote or for employees with a documented exception; they have no weekly office minimum but attend the company offsite and up to 8 planned office days per year.


## 2. Hours and availability
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in the employee’s contracted timezone, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. Outside those hours, asynchronous work is expected; messages after 19:00 local time should be scheduled unless there is a severity-1 incident. Employees must be reachable on the company chat tool during core hours except when on approved leave.

Remote and hybrid work is not a substitute for childcare or full-time caregiving during core hours. Occasional flexibility is encouraged; a standing conflict should be discussed with the manager and, if needed, with People Operations under the Leave Policy.


## 3. Workspace and equipment
IT issues a laptop, headset, and access to cloud systems. Hybrid employees may use a locker in the office; they should not leave laptops overnight in cars. Remote-band employees may claim home-office equipment under the Expense Policy (chair and desk caps). The company does not pay rent, utilities, or a generic “work from home stipend.”

The workspace must allow confidential calls without household members routinely hearing client names, and must have a lockable screen when the employee steps away. Public cafés may be used for Internal work; Confidential and Restricted work (see Data Handling Policy) must not be done on untrusted networks without the company VPN.


## 4. Meetings and travel
Default for internal meetings of more than four people is video with a shared agenda. Cameras are encouraged but not mandatory except for interviews and client presentations. Hybrid teams should not run “office-only” discussions that exclude remote colleagues; decisions belong in the team channel or ticket.

Travel to the designated office for Hybrid employees is commuting and is not reimbursed. Travel to another Acme office or a client site is business travel under the Expense Policy and should be booked when the in-person value is clear (workshops, incidents, sales). Managers must not require last-minute long-distance travel that forces the employee to miss statutory leave.


## 5. Performance and presence
Presence in an office is not a performance rating. Output, reliability, and collaboration are. Managers who systematically schedule important meetings only on days a remote colleague cannot attend, or who comment on camera backgrounds in a way that shames someone’s home, are in breach of the Code of Conduct.

If a Hybrid employee’s quarterly office average falls below 2 days per week without an approved exception (disability adjustment, temporary caring responsibility, or a documented project at a client site), the manager should first check for a legitimate barrier, then agree a plan. Repeated refusal without a reason can become a conduct process; it is not an automatic dismissal.


## 6. Health, safety, and emergencies
Employees must complete the annual home-working self-assessment in the HR system. Report accidents during working time to People Operations even if they happen at home. In a severe weather or transport shutdown, Hybrid employees should work remotely that day without burning leave.

Acme Corp may require a temporary full-remote or full-office period for a security incident or office closure. Those periods do not permanently change the employee’s band.

People Operations owns this policy. Local works councils or mandatory hybrid laws override it where they are stricter.
