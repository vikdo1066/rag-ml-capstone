# Acme Corp Expense and Reimbursement Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026. Employees may be reimbursed for reasonable, necessary business expenses incurred while performing work for Acme Corp. This policy applies to employees and to contractors who have been told in writing to use the company expense tool. Personal spending, commuting from home to a contracted office, and fines are never reimbursable.


## 1. How to claim
Claims must be submitted in the expense system within 30 days of the spend date, with an itemised receipt. Card-terminal slips without a breakdown are not sufficient for meals or hotels. Missing receipts require a written explanation and manager approval; more than two missing-receipt claims in a quarter will be rejected. Approvers must be one level above the claimant and must not approve their own costs. Finance pays approved claims weekly. Amounts above £500 require a director’s approval in addition to the line manager.

Company cards are for business spend only. Personal use of a company card must be repaid within 10 working days. Repeated personal use is a Code of Conduct issue.


## 2. Travel
Book the lowest logical fare that arrives in time for the meeting. Economy is the default for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy may be used for flights of 6 hours or more with director approval. Business class is not reimbursed except where a documented medical need exists. Rail should be used for journeys under 4 hours door to door in the UK and Western Europe.

Hotels should be 3-star equivalent, capped at £180 per night in London, £140 in other UK cities, and €200 in continental Europe, excluding tax. Higher caps require pre-approval. Ride-hailing and taxis are allowed to or from airports, late-night travel after 21:00, or when public transport is unsafe or impractical. Parking at the office is not reimbursed.

Loyalty points earned on business travel belong to the employee but itineraries must not be chosen to maximise points at the company’s expense.


## 3. Meals and entertainment
When travelling overnight, employees may claim breakfast up to £12, lunch up to £18, and dinner up to £35, including soft drinks and tax, excluding alcohol unless a client is present. Alcohol with a client is capped at £15 per person and two drinks. Team meals at the employee’s home location are not reimbursed unless they are a scheduled offsite with pre-approval.

Client entertainment must have a documented business purpose and the names of attendees. Gifts to clients or vendors over £50 require Legal review. Gifts from vendors over £25 must be declared to People Operations.


## 4. Software, hardware, and subscriptions
Laptops, phones, and monitors are provided by IT. Employees must not buy hardware and claim it back unless IT has issued a written exception (for example, a same-day replacement while travelling). Software and SaaS subscriptions must go through the procurement catalogue or a ticket to IT. Shadow IT—paying for an unapproved tool on a personal card—will not be reimbursed and may violate the Data Handling Policy if company data is stored there.

Home-office equipment for approved remote workers may be claimed once every 36 months up to £250 for a chair and £200 for a desk, with receipts. Consumables such as paper and ink are not reimbursed.


## 5. Training and professional memberships
Job-related training that is in the development plan is paid from the team training budget after manager approval. Optional certifications are reimbursed at 50 percent up to £400 per year if the employee remains employed for 12 months after the exam date; otherwise the amount is recovered from final pay where legally permitted. One professional membership per year is reimbursable up to £200 if the membership is required for the role.


## 6. What is never reimbursed
Examples: speeding and parking fines, minibar alcohol, in-room movies, clothing (except branded event apparel issued by Marketing), childcare, pet boarding, travel insurance that duplicates the company policy, political donations, and any spend that creates an undeclared conflict of interest.

Fraudulent claims will be treated as gross misconduct. Employees should ask finance-ops@acme.example before spending if a category is unclear.
